Electronics, From Zero to a Crumb-Collecting Robot

Starting at “what is voltage” and ending with a small autonomous robot that sweeps the dining table — understanding every wire in it along the way.
The arc Foundations first (Ohm's law → dividers → power), then the parts that disobey them (diodes → transistors), then control (logic → PWM → motor drivers), then sensing (including the edge sensor that keeps the robot on the table), and finally the build. Everything is taught through in-browser simulation until the fundamentals justify buying hardware — the course will say clearly when that moment arrives.

Lessons

  1. 01Voltage, Current, and ResistanceThree quantities and one equation — predict the current in a circuit before you build it.
  2. 02Series, Parallel, and the Voltage DividerHow voltage and current split between parts — and the two-resistor circuit hiding inside most sensors.
  3. 03Power, Batteries, and HeatWatts, amp-hours, and how long the robot actually runs before it stops.
