# Mission: Play Jazz Piano for Myself

## Why
Ricardo wants to be able to sit down at a piano and play jazz — for his own
enjoyment, with no pressure to perform for or with anyone else. He is a
complete beginner: no prior piano background, and no instrument yet. This is
a long-term, personal pursuit rather than a goal with a deadline.

## Success looks like
- Comfortably finds any note on the keyboard by pattern (black-key groups), not by counting from scratch every time.
- Plays basic jazz 7th-chord voicings (maj7, min7, dom7) confidently with either hand.
- Reads a simple lead sheet (melody + chord symbols) for an easy standard.
- Understands and can play a ii–V–I progression in a few common keys — the core building block of jazz harmony.
- Can sit down and play all the way through at least one simple jazz standard (e.g. "Autumn Leaves," "Fly Me to the Moon"), chords and melody together, solo.
- Has a beginning feel for improvising a simple line over a standard's changes — not polished, just personally satisfying.

## Constraints
- Complete beginner — no piano/keyboard experience, no music-reading experience assumed.
- No instrument yet. Early lessons need to work with an interactive on-screen keyboard; a real instrument (recommend at minimum a touch-sensitive 61-key keyboard, ideally weighted) should be acquired soon to make practice stick.
- Self-taught, solo learner — no teacher, no jam sessions planned. Learning happens through these lessons plus outside practice.
- Open-ended pace. No deadline — optimize for steady progress and long-term retention over speed.
- Learning style: skills-heavy topic. Lean on interactive practice and retrieval, keep knowledge/theory minimal and only as much as unlocks the next skill.

## Out of scope
- Playing with other musicians / jam sessions (not the goal right now).
- Classical piano technique and repertoire, except where it's genuinely a prerequisite (e.g. hand position, note reading basics).
- Deep jazz theory (modes, reharmonization, advanced substitutions) until the fundamentals above are solid.
- Sight-reading fluency — lead-sheet literacy (chord symbols + simple melody) is enough for this mission.
