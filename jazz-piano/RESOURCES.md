# Jazz Piano Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Hal Leonard Jazz Piano Method_ (Book 1) — various authors, Hal Leonard](https://www.halleonard.com/)
  The best-structured starting point for a true beginner. Covers hand position, chords/voicings, comping, swing feel, reading a lead sheet, and simple improvisation, with audio play-alongs. Use for: the backbone method to follow lesson-by-lesson while starting from zero.
- [Book: _The Jazz Piano Book_ — Mark Levine (Sher Music Co.)](https://www.shermusic.com/collections/mark-levine-collection)
  The classic, most-recommended jazz piano text — but written for musicians who can already read and play, not absolute beginners. Use for: **later**, once basic keyboard fluency and simple chords are solid; then it's the best next step for voicings, ii-V-I, and reharmonization.
- [YouTube: Piano With Jonny](https://www.youtube.com/@PianoWithJonny)
  A channel specifically built around jazz/pop piano for hobbyists, with clear breakdowns of voicings, comping patterns, and chord progressions. Use for: applied, "play this today" style lessons once basic chords are known.
- [YouTube: Aimee Nolte Music](https://www.youtube.com/@AimeeNolteMusic)
  A working jazz pianist who explains harmony and technique in an unusually clear, practical way, and shows how to apply theory immediately at the keyboard. Use for: jazz harmony explanations (ii-V-I, voicings, comping) once fundamentals are in place.
- [YouTube: Hoffman Academy](https://www.youtube.com/@HoffmanAcademy)
  Widely recommended for absolute piano fundamentals — hand position, note reading, keyboard geography — taught simply without being dumbed down. Use for: the very first steps (this is not jazz-specific, but the fundamentals transfer directly).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/piano](https://www.reddit.com/r/piano/)
  Large, active subreddit for piano learners generally. Use for: general beginner questions, gear questions, motivation.
- [r/Jazzpiano](https://www.reddit.com/r/Jazzpiano/) / [r/Jazz](https://www.reddit.com/r/Jazz/)
  More specific jazz piano and jazz listening communities. Use for: once past absolute basics — repertoire recommendations, listening suggestions, troubleshooting specific voicings.

**Note:** Ricardo's stated goal is playing solo for his own enjoyment, not jamming with others — communities are listed here as optional, low-pressure resources (mainly for questions and listening recommendations), not as a push toward group playing. Revisit if that preference changes.
